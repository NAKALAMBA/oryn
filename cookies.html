The Cookie
Collection

Three artisan cookies, each with a character entirely their own. Designed not just to be eaten, but to be savoured — with intention, with company, or in solitary delight.

"The best cookie is one that makes you stop
what you're doing."

We set out to make cookies that earn that pause. Each of our three flavours was developed through months of testing — balancing texture, flavour depth, and the kind of quality that only handcraft can achieve.

01

Aurelia

Named after the Latin word for golden, Aurelia is the cookie for those who appreciate depth.

Named after the Latin word for "golden," Aurelia is a cookie that celebrates warmth and quiet elegance. It opens with the vibrant brightness of fresh orange zest before unfolding into comforting layers of cinnamon, ginger, and nutmeg—rich, aromatic, and beautifully balanced.

Each bite lingers with buttery depth and gentle spice, making Aurelia a cookie that's both familiar and refined, crafted for those who appreciate subtle complexity.

Flavour Notes

ORANGE ZEST CINNAMON GINGER NUTMEG

02

Jade Caramel

Green and gold. Earthy and sweet. The Jade Caramel is the most intellectually interesting cookie we make.

Green and gold. Earthy and sweet. The Jade Caramel is the most intellectually interesting cookie we make. Matcha and butterscotch shouldn't work together — and yet, in the right proportions and with the right technique, they create something that is better than either alone.

Dense, chewy, with pockets of butterscotch caramel that melt in the warmth of your mouth — the Jade Caramel is the cookie that surprises you every time, even when you already know to expect it.

Flavour Notes

03

Cloud Crumb

There is a reason certain things become classics.

There is a reason some cookies become classics. Cloud Crumb earns its name with a soft, delicate crumb that melts on the first bite. Rich dark chocolate creates molten pockets, while toasted mini marshmallows add a nostalgic sweetness that lingers long after.

Simple in appearance, thoughtful in every detail. Made with real butter, premium dark chocolate, and toasted marshmallows folded into a buttery vanilla dough, every bite is soft, comforting, and impossible to resist.
