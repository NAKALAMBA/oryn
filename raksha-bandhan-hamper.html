The Story

A Box Built for Rakhi

"The best Rakhi gifts aren't just sweet — they're remembered."

Every Rakhi brings the same small dilemma: something sweet, yes, but something that actually feels chosen. Not a box picked up on the way, but one that says a sibling thought about what the other would genuinely like.

The Classic Cookie Box exists for exactly that moment. Two cookies each of Aurelia, Jade Caramel, and Cloud Crumb — three distinct personalities in one box, so there's something for every sibling's taste, whether they lean toward warm spice, earthy caramel, or soft chocolate.

It arrives in our signature kraft box, finished with a sticker carrying tasting notes for each flavour, and a handwritten personalised message on request — ready to give as it is, no extra wrapping needed.

And because Rakhi doesn't always mean being in the same city, every box ships free across India, packed to travel so the distance never gets in the way of the celebration.
