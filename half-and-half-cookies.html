The Story

Why Choose One When You Can Have Two?

"Made for curious taste buds, late-night cravings, and anyone who believes the best things come in pairs."

The 1/2 & 1/2 Cookie began with a simple, slightly indulgent question: why settle for one flavour when a single cookie could carry two? We built each one around a contrast — two distinct doughs joined down the middle, baked together so neither half loses its character.

Solstice is the bright one. Half fresh, citrusy lemon; half juicy, fruity blueberry. Together they land somewhere zesty and beautifully balanced — a little citrus, a little berry, and a whole lot of sunshine.

Eclipse is the rich, playful one. On one side, deep, indulgent dark chocolate. On the other, nostalgic vanilla confetti — sweet, creamy, and scattered with colour. Every cookie is a delicious back-and-forth between bold and playful.

And when choosing favourites feels like too much responsibility, there is the Assorted Box — four Solstice and four Eclipse, so you don't have to choose sides at all.

Every box holds eight cookies, freshly baked and carefully packed. ₹1,000 a box, with shipping included across India.
