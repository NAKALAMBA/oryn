The Story Behind Saanjh

"Saanjh is not a product. It is a feeling. The feeling of coming home."

सांझ is the Hindi word for evening—but it means more than a time of day. It is that quiet hour when the day's rush begins to soften. The kettle goes on, conversations slow down, and a simple cup of chai becomes a small ritual of comfort.

We created this financier to belong to that moment.

Built on deeply browned butter and infused with a concentrated Assam tea reduction, सांझ unfolds in layers. Toasted milk lends a mellow richness, while a caramelized oat praline adds warmth, texture, and a gentle crunch. Every element is chosen to echo the familiar comforts of buttered toast and evening tea, without ever overpowering them.

A financier is a classic French tea cake, prized for its crisp edges and tender, buttery centre. Our interpretation carries that tradition forward while embracing flavours that feel unmistakably at home with Indian chai.

सांझ isn't just a pastry to be enjoyed with tea—it is crafted for the quiet pause that tea invites. A little moment of stillness, wrapped in brown butter, toasted grains, and the warmth of dusk.
