A Delhi Love Story

Not every love story is meant to last. Some exist for a season. Some for a chapter. Some become memories that quietly stay with us forever. Through five thoughtfully crafted flavours, we translate emotions into taste, texture, and experience.

Inspired by the timeless charm of Delhi, this collection celebrates the moments that stay with us long after they pass. Each muffin is thoughtfully handcrafted to capture a feeling, a memory, and a flavour worth returning to, inviting you to savour every bite with intention.

Citrus Spice Bloom

Not every love story has a beginning quite like this one.

Every love story has a beginning. And there is nothing quite like the feeling of that first meeting. Bright, unexpected, warm yet sharp — like the first time you taste something that feels both new and familiar at once.

The Citrus Spice Bloom is our take on that first flutter. It opens with the brightness of citrus, tempered by the warmth of spice. It is bold but not overwhelming. Optimistic. Something that makes you want to return for another bite.

This is the chapter where things get complex.

This is the chapter where things get complex. The chapter where two very different personalities find each other fascinating. Slightly earthy and sophisticated meets golden, caramelised sweetness.

The Matcha Butterscotch is our most intellectually interesting muffin. It shouldn't work — the bitterness of matcha against the richness of butterscotch — but it does, magnificently. Like the best relationships, it is better for its contradictions.
