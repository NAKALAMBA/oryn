Flavours of
India

India is not one flavour. It is a thousand. We spent time in its kitchens, its markets, its ceremonies — and came back with three desserts that try to do justice to the richness of what we found.

India has a culinary vocabulary that the world is only beginning to understand.

Every region carries centuries of technique, ingredient knowledge, and cultural nuance. The way Kerala uses coconut and spice is entirely different from how Kolkata uses date palm jaggery. Gujarat's relationship with sweetness is unlike any other cuisine on earth.

We asked: what happens when these traditions meet the vocabulary of contemporary patisserie? Not fusion — translation. Respectful, considered, technically precise translation.

Kerala · Malabar Coast

Malabar Gold
Éclair

Set of 3 — ₹600

Kerala's Malabar coast is one of the world's great spice regions.

For centuries, Kerala's Malabar Coast was renowned for its spices, coconuts, and palm jaggery—treasures that travelled the ancient spice routes and shaped cuisines around the world.

Our Malabar Gold Éclair pays homage to this rich heritage. A delicate cardamom choux is filled with a light banana, coconut, and jaggery diplomat, layered with toasted coconut-cashew praline, and finished with a glossy salted jaggery glaze—a refined tribute to the timeless flavours of Kerala.

Kolkata · City of Joy

Nolen Halo
Cupcake

Set of 4 — ₹600

Nolen gur is sweet in a way that refined sugar cannot replicate.

For generations, nolen gur has been the flavour of winter in West Bengal—the prized date palm jaggery that finds its way into cherished desserts like mishti doi, payesh, and patishapta. Its sweetness is unlike refined sugar: deep, caramel-like, gently smoky, and unmistakably seasonal.

The Nolen Halo Cupcake is a tribute to this extraordinary ingredient. A soft, golden spiced sponge infused with nolen gur, crowned with a silky nolen gur ermine cream and finished with a delicate drizzle of nolen gur capturing the warmth, nostalgia, and quiet elegance of a Bengali winter in every bite.

Gujarat · Land of Colours

Apricus Bento
Cake

₹450 per box

Gujarat is India's most colourful state — both literally and gastronomically.

Gujarat's cuisine is a celebration of warmth—where ripe mango, roasted grains, fragrant spices, and rich textures come together in effortless harmony.

Our Oryn Signature: Apricus Bento—apricus, Latin for warmed by the sun—captures this golden spirit in a refined individual cake. Toasted besan sponge, sun-ripened mango, saffron cream, and crisp gond praline come together in a modern tribute to Gujarat's timeless flavours.
