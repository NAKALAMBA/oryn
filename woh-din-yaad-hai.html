Nostalgia Collection

WOH DIN -
Yaad Hai?

A Box Full of the Good Old Days.

Collection Story

Some memories never
really fade.

They live in the excitement of opening a steel tiffin before the school bell rang, in the comforting aroma of something freshly baked after school, in rainy afternoons spent with a warm glass of milk, and in quiet evenings where the simplest moments became the most unforgettable.

WOH DIN - Yaad Hai? is ORYN's heartfelt tribute to those memories.

Inspired by the warmth of childhood and handcrafted with premium ingredients, this collection celebrates the little rituals that shaped us. Every product tells a different story, while one timeless ingredient- almond- ties them all together. A symbol of comfort, nourishment, and home, almonds have been part of our childhood for generations, and this collection reimagines those familiar flavours through handcrafted artisanal bakes.

More than just desserts, these are memories waiting to be relived- one bite at a time.

The Thread That Ties It Together

Almond as memory, comfort, and home.

Across every bake in this collection, almond returns as the one timeless note. It brings nourishment, familiarity, and a quiet sense of home to each chapter while letting every dessert carry its own distinct story.
